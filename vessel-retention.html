Separation time uses Stokes' Law — 200-micron average droplet, 100°F water viscosity (0.68 cP). Settling distance is the water column: the bottom 90% of the liquid depth, or with Oil & Water open, the depth below the interface. Hotter fluid separates faster. Colder fluid separates slower.

Assumes discrete droplets. Emulsified or chemically stabilized fluids will not separate gravitationally regardless of vessel size. Use with caution below 20° API — density contrast with water becomes minimal.

Ballpark estimates only. Not a substitute for engineering design or lab characterization.
